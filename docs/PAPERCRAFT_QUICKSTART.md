# Paper Craft Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 Create Your Own Magic Gem in 5 Minutes!

### What You'll Need

- 🖨️ Printer
- 📄 Card stock paper (110lb weight recommended)
- ✂️ Scissors or craft knife
- 📏 Ruler (for scoring)
- 🖊️ Empty ballpoint pen (for scoring)
- 🧴 Glue stick or white glue
- ⏱️ 10-15 minutes

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### 1. Generate Your Template

1. Open the web app: http://localhost:8880
2. Click the **"📄 Paper Nets"** tab
3. Select any magic square from the gallery (left sidebar)
4. The template appears on the right!

#### 2. Customize (Optional)

Toggle these options as desired:
- ✅ **Show Glue Tabs** - Recommended ON
- ✅ **Show Cell Values** - Shows magic square numbers
- ✅ **Show Instructions** - Helpful for first time

Click **"Color Similar Faces"** to group faces by shape (helpful!)

#### 3. Print or Download

**Option A: Print Directly**
- Click **"🖨️ Print"** button
- Select your printer
- Use **100% scale** (no fit-to-page)
- Print!

**Option B: Download First**
- Click **"📥 Download PNG"** button
- Open the saved image
- Print from your image viewer

💡 **Tip**: Card stock makes sturdier models!

#### 4. Prepare Your Template

1. **Score the fold lines** (dashed):
   - Place ruler along each dashed line
   - Run empty pen along ruler firmly
   - This creates a crease for easy folding

2. **Cut along solid lines** (black):
   - Cut carefully around the entire perimeter
   - Include the gray glue tabs
   - Keep all pieces connected!

#### 5. Assemble Your Magic Gem

1. **Start folding**:
   - Fold along all dashed lines
   - Gently pre-crease each fold
   - Shape will start to form

2. **Glue the tabs**:
   - Apply glue to ONE gray tab
   - Press against the matching edge
   - Hold for 10-20 seconds
   - Let dry
   - Repeat for each tab

3. **Work systematically**:
   - Glue adjacent faces first
   - Build up the shape gradually
   - Last few tabs are trickiest - be patient!

#### 6. Admire Your Creation! ✨

You've just created a 3D geometric representation of a magic square!

### 🎓 What You're Holding

- **Convex Hull**: The outer surface of all magic square points
- **Faces**: Each triangle is determined by three magic square cells
- **Colors**: Same-colored faces have identical geometry
- **Values**: Numbers on vertices show the magic square

### 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Better Folds**: Score before folding for crisp edges
2. **Cleaner Gluing**: Use less glue than you think
3. **Patience**: Work slowly, especially on the last tabs
4. **Mistakes**: If glue is still wet, gently pull apart and retry
5. **Display**: Once complete, your model is surprisingly sturdy!

### 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Q: The tabs don't seem to match up.**
- A: This is normal for complex shapes. Some tabs may need slight overlap.

**Q: The last tab won't close.**
- A: Leave it open if needed - the structure is still stable.

**Q: The shape looks different from the 3D viewer.**
- A: The net preserves distances but appears flat - trust the folds!

**Q: Can I make a bigger/smaller model?**
- A: Yes! Use print scaling (200% for 2× size) or download and resize in image editor.

**Q: The paper is too thin.**
- A: Try card stock (110-300 gsm) for much sturdier models.
